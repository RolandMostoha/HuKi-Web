Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-07

This Privacy Policy explains how HuKi processes personal data when you use the iOS and Android app. HuKi is a Hungarian hiking app that offers map views, place search, GPX import, and place history features.

1. Data controller

The data controller is Roland Mostoha, acting as an individual developer.

Privacy contact: huki.app@gmail.com

No postal address is currently published. Privacy requests can be sent by email.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to the processing of personal data in connection with the HuKi app. HuKi does not operate user accounts, does not require sign-in, does not sell personal data, and does not display in-app advertising.

HuKi can be supported voluntarily with one-time purchases or monthly subscriptions through the App Store or Google Play. Support unlocks no additional features: every feature of HuKi stays free for everyone. As a thank-you, supporters get a badge with their chosen forest animal, which appears in places inside the app, such as the menu and the Support screen.

HuKi does not track you across other apps or websites and does not use advertising identifiers (such as IDFA). HuKi does not display an App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not collect data for advertising or targeting purposes.

HuKi uses location data only while the app is actively in use. HuKi does not perform background location tracking.

3. What data HuKi may process

  • Location data: precise or approximate location data if you grant location permission.
  • Search queries: the text you enter when using online place search.
  • GPX files: GPX files you import and related metadata.
  • Place history: a local history of places you opened in the app.
  • Diagnostic and crash data: technical information related to crashes, errors, and app stability.
  • Analytics data: aggregated usage events and app interaction statistics.
  • Technical network and device data: IP address, installation identifiers, operating system, app version, and similar data when required by map, search, or diagnostics infrastructure.
  • Purchase data: if you support HuKi, which support packages you purchased, your subscription status, and the purchase identifiers provided by the App Store or Google Play. HuKi never receives your payment card details, your name, or your Apple or Google account.
  • Email correspondence: your email address and the content of your message, if you contact HuKi by email.

4. Why HuKi uses data

  • to show your position on the map;
  • to improve nearby and relevant hiking destination suggestions;
  • to provide online place search and autocomplete results;
  • to render maps and route-related content;
  • to plan routes between the points you choose in the Route Planner;
  • to import, read, manage, and keep GPX files on your device;
  • to remember recent places and GPX history locally on the device;
  • to process voluntary support purchases, show your supporter badge, and restore your purchases after reinstalling the app or on a new device;
  • to answer your emails;
  • to diagnose errors, improve app stability, and understand usage trends.

5. Legal bases

Where the GDPR applies, HuKi may process personal data on the following legal bases:

  • consent or operating system permission, especially for location access;
  • performance of the requested service, when HuKi provides features you actively use, such as maps, search, or GPX handling;
  • performance of a contract, when you make a voluntary support purchase;
  • legitimate interests, for app security, reliability, debugging, product improvement, and answering your emails.

6. Location data

HuKi uses location data only while the app is open and actively used. Location access supports features such as showing your position on the map, ranking nearby hiking destinations more helpfully, and supporting search and map interactions.

HuKi does not use background location, and it does not continue tracking your movement after the app is closed.

You can revoke location permission at any time in iOS or Android system settings.

7. Data stored on your device

HuKi stores several types of data locally on your device:

  • GPX files: imported routes are copied into the app's own storage and remain there until you delete them.
  • GPX metadata: such as file name, last opened time, distance, and estimated travel time.
  • Place history: locally stored records of places you previously opened.
  • Settings: app preferences and configuration values.
  • Supporter records: the support packages you purchased, their purchase identifiers, and the supporter animal you chose.

On Android, the app settings, including the supporter records, may be included in your device's Android backup (Google cloud backup and device-to-device transfer), so your supporter badge is kept when you restore or move to a new device. GPX files and place history are not included in this backup.

HuKi primarily manages this data on-device. You can remove it from within the app where supported, by clearing app data, or by uninstalling the app, depending on the data type.

8. Third-party services and processors

HuKi may use the following third-party services:

  • Mapbox for maps and location-related infrastructure. More information: Mapbox Privacy Policy
  • LocationIQ for online place autocomplete and geocoding-related search. More information: LocationIQ Privacy Policy
  • GraphHopper for route planning. The points of the route you plan are sent to calculate the route. More information: GraphHopper Privacy Policy
  • HuKi-Routing, HuKi's own route planning server for routes within Hungary, which may be used instead of GraphHopper. It runs on Google Cloud (Cloud Run, Belgium, EU) and is a stateless routing engine: it receives the points of the route you plan, calculates the route and returns it to the app. HuKi-Routing never stores your route points or any other location. Google Cloud only keeps technical request logs without coordinates (time, response status, IP address) for up to 30 days. More information: Google Cloud Privacy Notice
  • Apple App Store and Google Play for processing voluntary support purchases and subscriptions. Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google; HuKi only receives the purchase result. More information: Apple Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Analytics for aggregated app usage analytics, including support-related events (such as which support package was selected or purchased, with its price and currency where the store reports it) and your supporter level. No payment details or account data are sent. More information: Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Firebase Crashlytics for crash reporting and diagnostics. More information: Firebase Privacy and Security

These providers may also process data under their own privacy notices and service terms.

9. Retention

  • GPX files and related local metadata: remain on your device until you delete them.
  • Place history: remains on your device until app data is cleared or the feature later offers dedicated deletion controls.
  • Firebase Crashlytics data: according to Firebase's official privacy documentation, crash stack traces and related identifiers are generally retained for 90 days before removal begins from live and backup systems.
  • HuKi-Routing route planning requests: route points are not stored; technical request logs without coordinates are kept by Google Cloud for up to 30 days.
  • Supporter records: remain on your device (and in your Android backup, if enabled) until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Apple and Google keep their own transaction records according to their policies.
  • Emails: kept only as long as needed to answer and follow up on your message.
  • Analytics and other third-party data: are retained according to the provider's own retention settings and policies.

10. International transfers

Some third-party providers, especially international cloud and analytics providers, may process personal data outside Hungary or the European Economic Area. Where this happens, data is processed subject to the provider's applicable legal transfer mechanisms and safeguards.

11. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • request information about data processing;
  • request access to your personal data;
  • request correction or deletion where applicable;
  • object to certain processing;
  • request restriction of processing where legally available;
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To submit a privacy request, email huki.app@gmail.com.

If you are in Hungary, you may also contact the National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH): www.naih.hu

12. How to manage or delete your data

  • revoke location permission in system settings;
  • delete imported GPX files from the app;
  • clear app data in your device settings;
  • uninstall the app from your device;
  • cancel a support subscription at any time in the App Store or Google Play subscription settings; refunds are handled by Apple or Google;
  • email HuKi about data processed by external services where applicable.

13. Children

HuKi is a general-audience app and is not specifically directed to children. HuKi does not knowingly seek to collect personal data from children.

14. Data security

HuKi applies reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can guarantee absolute security.

15. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time, for example when HuKi adds new features, new processors, or when legal requirements change. The current version will always be available on this page.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, email huki.app@gmail.com.
