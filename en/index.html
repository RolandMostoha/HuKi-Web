Hungarian Hiking Map

HuKi is a mobile app for outdoor enthusiasts, built around Hungary's hiking trail markers. Its goal is to promote the exploration of Hungary's natural beauty while being a helpful companion on our adventures. HuKi is free, ad-free, and open-source software, built and maintained by a single developer in their spare time as a hobby project.

map

Hiking Trails

The application uses the Hungarian Hike layer with the national hiking trails, which is integrated with the base map.


navigation

Navigation

In navigation mode, HuKi follows your position and continuously shows the remaining distance and walking time to each stop.


Route Planner

With the route planner, we can plan routes on hiking trails. The application estimates the length and time of the tour and creates a GPX file.


GPX Tracks

With HuKi, GPX files can be imported and displayed on the map. The app provides distance, time estimation, and elevation coloring.


landscape

Landscapes

HuKi shows the hungarian landscapes on a map view, with their associated popular excursion places and hiking collections.


OKT - National Blue Trail

For blue trail hikers, a feature is available where the OKT, RPDDK, AKT blue rail sections and stamp locations can also be viewed.
